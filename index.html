COMMON QUESTIONS

Useful answers before you start.

What kind of reporting workflows does ReportDrafter support?

ReportDrafter is designed for recurring professional reports built from source documents, templates and historical examples — including finance, fund, risk, compliance, operations and consulting workflows.

Can we use our existing report templates?

Yes. The workflow is designed around your existing report structure so your team can keep the format and review process it already knows.

How does human review work?

Generated content can be reviewed alongside confidence indicators, source evidence and tracked changes before the report is finalised.

Can individuals use ReportDrafter without a team?

Yes. Individuals and small teams can start with a self-serve workspace, while larger organisations can discuss shared workspaces and reporting operations with the team.

How can I discuss security or an enterprise deployment?

Use the contact page to start a conversation about your data handling, access controls, integrations and deployment requirements.
